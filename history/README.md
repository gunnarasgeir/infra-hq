# Change history

Copies of settings as they were **before** they were changed or removed, with a short description. Newest entry first.

> **For the agent.** Before deleting or changing DNS records, access rules, firewall rules or other settings, save the current state here as `YYYY-MM-DD-<description>.json` (or `.yaml`, `.conf`, ...) with secrets removed, and add an entry below. The change itself shows in the commit.
